The Estate-Wide Documentary

THE WHOLE STORY. FROM THE BEGINNING.

The first installment of From Land to Legacy is conceived as an introduction to the entire estate. It will begin with approved aerial views of the landscape and its present condition, move into a public-safe conceptual master-plan animation, introduce the agricultural and visitor ambitions, explain how systems connect, and invite people to follow the journey.

The documentary is in development. No video or completed construction is represented here as already released.

The Open Farm Project

LEARN TOGETHER. SOLVE REAL CHALLENGES.

The Open Farm Project is the technical participation channel within the wider documentary effort. As specific research questions are prepared for publication, students, engineers, agricultural professionals and other qualified contributors will be invited to explore bounded problems and share ideas.

Participation briefs will be released only when they are ready; there is no active challenge submission process on this page yet.
