Development Partnerships

Tidal Crest Group is exploring opportunities to support state-level development initiatives across Nigeria through structured, capital-backed partnerships. Our current focus includes engagement with Kwara State around revenue assurance, land administration digitization, agricultural investment, and diaspora capital pathways — areas central to the state’s ongoing fiscal and economic development priorities.

This work reflects Tidal Crest’s broader mandate: deploying private capital and operational expertise toward initiatives that generate sustainable value for communities while building long-term institutional legacy for the Group and its partners.

Areas of Interest

Revenue Assurance & Fiscal Transparency

Supporting more accurate, real-time visibility into public revenue collection.

Land Administration Digitization

Modernizing registry and titling infrastructure to unlock land value and investment confidence.

Agricultural Investment & Export Development

Through Tidal Crest Agro, structuring aggregation and value-chain financing models that connect smallholder farmers to processors and export markets.

Diaspora Capital Mobilization

Building structured pathways for diaspora investors to participate meaningfully in state-level development.

Institutional Approach

PRESERVE. BUILD. ENDURE.

Tidal Crest Group approaches this work through proper institutional channels, in partnership with relevant government bodies, and in line with its founding principles.
