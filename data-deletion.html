Data Retention & Deletion

Private household data controls

Authorized users can disconnect financial institutions and remove Plaid-derived information from Olayori Central's active storage.

How to Delete Plaid-Derived Data

Within the private Olayori Central application, an authorized household user can open System → Privacy & Data and use the Disconnect Plaid & delete active Plaid data control. Sensitive actions require authentication or recent verification.

What the Deletion Action Does

The action disconnects the applicable Plaid connection and removes associated Plaid-derived information from the active application database. A future connection requires a new authorization and acceptance of the then-current policy where applicable.

Encrypted Recovery Backups

Olayori Central maintains encrypted recovery backups for disaster recovery. Deleted active data may remain in an older encrypted backup until that backup expires under the application's retention schedule. Recovery backups are not used as an alternate active data store.

Retention Schedule

Plaid-derived active dataRetained while the connection remains authorized and the data is needed for the requested household functionality; removed from active storage on disconnect/deletion.
Plaid connection tokensRetained only while needed to maintain an authorized connection; removed when the connection is disconnected or deleted.
Encrypted recovery backupsRotated and expired according to the application's backup-retention schedule.
Security and consent recordsRetained only as reasonably necessary for accountability, security, consent history or applicable legal requirements.

Other Household Records

User-created documents, ideas, notes and household records remain under the control of authorized household users and may be removed through the applicable application functions. Requests that cannot be completed through the application can be submitted through the Tidal Crest contact page.

Questions or Requests

For privacy, deletion or retention questions concerning Olayori Central, use the Tidal Crest contact page. Requests may require verification before information is disclosed or deleted.

Olayori Central is a private household application and is not offered as a public financial service.
