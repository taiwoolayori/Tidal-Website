Olayori Central

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective August 16, 2026

This policy explains how Olayori Central handles information used for private household financial management.

1. Scope and Purpose

Olayori Central is a private household application operated in connection with Tidal Crest Group. It is not offered to the general public. The application helps authorized household users organize financial information, household records, projects and selected connected services.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features an authorized household user chooses to use, Olayori Central may handle account identifiers, balances, transaction information, investment information, liabilities, recurring transactions, consent records, security logs, documents, notes and other household records. Financial account information may be obtained through Plaid when an authorized user elects to connect an eligible financial institution.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only for authorized household purposes, including personal financial organization, account aggregation, budgeting, household planning, recordkeeping, security, troubleshooting and application improvement. Olayori Central does not sell household financial data and does not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Consent and Financial Connections

Before a new Plaid connection is initiated, an authorized household user is required to review and accept the current policy and consent to the collection, processing and storage of the information needed for the requested household functionality. Consent events are versioned and timestamped. Plaid and the connected financial institution may also present their own authorization and privacy notices.

5. Data Sharing

Information is shared only as necessary to operate requested integrations or services, comply with applicable law, protect the security and integrity of the application, or respond to a valid legal requirement. Olayori Central does not provide household financial information to outside advertisers or data brokers.

6. Security

Olayori Central uses access controls, passkey/WebAuthn authentication, encrypted storage for sensitive application payloads, security logging, and vulnerability-management practices appropriate to its private household deployment. No security method can guarantee absolute security, but controls are maintained and reviewed as the application changes.

7. Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only while reasonably needed for its authorized household purpose, security, recovery or applicable legal obligations. Plaid-derived active data can be deleted by disconnecting Plaid and invoking the application's deletion controls. Encrypted recovery backups may contain older copies until they expire under the backup-retention schedule. Additional details appear in the Data Retention and Deletion page.

8. Access and Data Rights

Authorized household users may review connected accounts, disconnect a financial institution, request deletion of Plaid-derived active data, and manage applicable household records through the private application. Privacy or data-handling questions may be directed through the Tidal Crest contact page.

9. Policy Changes

This policy is reviewed periodically and when material changes are made to Olayori Central's data practices, connected services, storage architecture or legal obligations. Material updates may require renewed acknowledgement or consent within the application.

10. Contact

For questions about Olayori Central privacy or household data practices, contact Tidal Crest Group through the contact page.

Important: Plaid maintains separate privacy practices for information processed within Plaid's systems. This policy describes Olayori Central's application-level practices.
